# Insight 4 Business

**Head of Cyber as a Service (vCISO).** Senior security leadership for companies that need a head of security without a full-time hire, so a customer's security questionnaire, an ISO 27001 audit or a due diligence stops blocking a deal.

## What is included

- Security strategy and roadmap
- Risk management and compliance readiness: ISO 27001, LGPD, GDPR and customer due diligence
- Oversight of security operations: SOC, incident response and vulnerability management
- Cloud and identity security architecture
- Executive reporting and answers to customer security questionnaires

Engagements run as a monthly retainer with a defined scope, or by project. To talk about your case, go to https://insight4biz.com.

## How we work

- **Assess**: a clear diagnosis of what exposes the business, with priorities.
- **Build**: the controls, detections and automation, as code.
- **Operate**: continuous oversight and response, led by the same person from diagnosis to operation.

## Open repositories: how we do the work

The procedures behind the service, rebuilt from scratch and free of client data:

- **[cyber-templates](https://github.com/insight4business/cyber-templates)**: the entry point and map of the repositories below.
- Nine process repositories with practical, vendor-neutral procedures organised by sub-process and technology: [security-operations](https://github.com/insight4business/security-operations), [detection-engineering](https://github.com/insight4business/detection-engineering), [data-protection](https://github.com/insight4business/data-protection), [devsecops](https://github.com/insight4business/devsecops), [governance](https://github.com/insight4business/governance), [threat-intelligence](https://github.com/insight4business/threat-intelligence), [offensive-security](https://github.com/insight4business/offensive-security), [artificial-intelligence](https://github.com/insight4business/artificial-intelligence) and [cloud-operations](https://github.com/insight4business/cloud-operations).

Everything is generic and uses fictional examples. No client data, ever. Review every template with your own legal and security teams before adopting.
