# Insight 4 Business

**AI and cybersecurity for companies.** We monitor, test and prioritize what exposes your data, and we use AI to detect fraud and loss before they turn into damage. Teams in Brazil and the United States.

**IA e cibersegurança para empresas.** Monitoramos, testamos e priorizamos o que expõe seus dados, e usamos IA para detectar fraude e perda antes que virem prejuízo. Operação no Brasil e nos EUA.

Website: https://insight4biz.com

## What we do

| Front | Focus |
|---|---|
| **Cybersecurity** | Security operations (SOC), detection engineering, governance, risk and compliance, vulnerability and identity management, data protection, penetration testing, threat intelligence |
| **Applied AI** | AI applied to business processes, AI chatbots, and security and governance for AI applications and agents |
| **Cloud operations** | Azure, AWS, Microsoft 365 and Google Cloud: engineering, security and cost, all as code |

## Our own product

**Alerto!** uses the cameras a store already has to point out theft and show what happens at the shop window. The AI alerts; your team decides. It is a private commercial product and is not part of our open repositories.
